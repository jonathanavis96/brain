## SECTION 3: GROUNDS FOR OBJECTION

We, the undersigned registered owners of Erf 117315 Cape Town (32 Rosmead Avenue Service Road, Claremont), hereby formally object to the GV2025 market valuation of **R 3,600,000** as reflected in the 2025 General Valuation Roll.

### 3.1 Basis of Objection

We submit that the GV2025 valuation of R 3,600,000 does not accurately reflect the market value of the subject property as at the prescribed valuation date of **1 July 2025**, and that the correct market value is closer to **R 3,200,000 – R 3,350,000**.

Our objection is grounded in the following:

1. **Location Discount — Service Road Frontage:** The property at 32 Rosmead Avenue is situated on a **service road**, not the main Rosmead Avenue carriageway. Properties fronting service roads typically attract a market discount of 5–10% compared to equivalent properties on conventional residential streets, due to reduced desirability, increased through-traffic noise, and lower kerb appeal.

2. **Comparable Sales Evidence:** Recent sales of comparable residential properties in Claremont and the immediate surrounds (within a 1km radius, sold within 12 months of 1 July 2025) indicate that properties of similar size (400–500 m² erf) and configuration are transacting in the range of **R 3,100,000 – R 3,500,000**, with the upper end reserved for properties with superior finishes, position, and no service road frontage.

3. **Erf Size vs. Value Ratio:** At 435 m², the property is a modest residential erf. The proposed valuation of R 3,600,000 implies a land rate of approximately **R 8,276/m²**, which is at the upper range for Claremont service road properties and is not supported by recent Deeds Office transfer data for comparable properties.

4. **No Structural Improvements Assessment:** The valuation appears to rely primarily on statistical modelling rather than a physical inspection. No account appears to have been taken of the specific value-reducing factors attributable to service road frontage and the property's physical configuration.
