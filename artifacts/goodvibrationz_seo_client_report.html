Introduction
What is SEO and why does it matter for you?

SEO stands for "Search Engine Optimisation." In plain English, it means making your website easier for Google to find and understand — so that when someone in Cape Town searches for "wedding DJ Cape Town" or "hire a DJ for my wedding," your website appears near the top of the results instead of a competitor's.

Your website looks great and has all the right information. The problem is that Google is currently struggling to understand what you do, where you are, and why couples should choose you. This report explains exactly what to fix and why it matters for your bookings.

A simple way to think about it

Your website is like your DJ booth — it looks and sounds incredible. But right now the venue doors are locked and there's no sign outside. SEO is the signage, the open doors, and the listing in every wedding directory that gets people through the door in the first place.

Term 1

Title Tags

The headline Google shows in search results. Like a shop sign — the first thing people see before deciding whether to click.

Term 2

Meta Descriptions

The short description under your title in Google results. Think of it as a two-sentence pitch that convinces someone to choose your link over a competitor's.

Term 3

Headings

The bold text on your pages. Google reads these to understand what each section is about — they're not just for visitors.

Term 4

Schema / Structured Data

Invisible code that tells Google exactly what your business is, where you're located, and what you charge. Unlocks enhanced features in search results.

Urgent

Your website has no Google snippet text

When someone searches for a wedding DJ in Cape Town, Google shows a short description under your website link. Right now that description is completely blank — so Google writes its own (usually badly), and fewer people click your link.

Now
Google makes something up — often the first sentence of your page, which may not be relevant or compelling.
Fix
Cape Town wedding & events DJ Mat Pheiffer brings professional sound, lighting & personalised playlists to your big day. Packages from R6,000. Get a free quote!
Why this matters for bookings This text is your advertisement in Google's search results. When a bride-to-be sees your listing next to a competitor's, this is what convinces her to click yours. Mentioning packages from R6,000 also attracts serious enquiries and filters out people who aren't ready to book.
Urgent

Your website title doesn't tell Google what you do

The title tag is the main blue link text in Google results and the most important signal Google uses to decide when to show your site. Currently it just shows your brand name — which Google doesn't know is a DJ business in Cape Town.

Now
Good Vibrationz
Fix
Good Vibrationz | Cape Town Wedding & Events DJ | DJ Mat Pheiffer
Why this matters for bookings When someone searches "wedding DJ Cape Town," Google doesn't know to show your site because those words aren't in your title. Adding them tells Google exactly where you are and what you do. This is the single biggest ranking signal on the entire page.
Urgent

Your main homepage heading misses a key opportunity

The big headline at the top of your homepage is another major signal to Google. It works well for visitors — but Google needs to see your location and service type in it to rank you for relevant searches.

Now
Unforgettable Entertainment for Your Wedding
Fix
Cape Town Wedding & Events DJ | Unforgettable Music by DJ Mat Pheiffer
Why this matters for bookings "Unforgettable Entertainment" sounds lovely but is invisible to search engines. "Cape Town Wedding DJ" is the exact phrase couples type into Google. Including it here helps your page rank for that search without changing how your site feels to read.

Important

Your pricing packages have vague names

Your packages are called "Basic," "Happy Medium," and "Going Big." These work well as friendly names for visitors, but they give Google nothing to work with when someone searches "wedding DJ packages Cape Town" or "DJ hire price Cape Town."

Now
BASIC  /  HAPPY MEDIUM  /  GOING BIG
Fix
Basic Wedding DJ Package – R6,000  /  Standard Wedding DJ Package – R9,500  /  Premium Wedding DJ Package – R17,500
Why this matters for bookings Including "Wedding DJ Package" and the price in the section headings means Google can sometimes show your pricing directly in search results as a featured snippet — at the very top of the page, above all other results. Couples who are price-comparing will find you before they even visit your site.
Important

Your Contact page has a technical conflict

Every webpage should have one main heading — like a chapter title in a book. Your Contact page accidentally has two main headings, which confuses Google about what the page is about.

Now
"Get in Touch" and "We'd Love to Hear from You!" are both set as the main heading.
Fix
Main heading: "Book DJ Mat for Your Cape Town Wedding or Event" — Sub-heading: "We'd Love to Hear from You"
Why this matters for bookings One clear main heading focused on booking helps Google rank your Contact page when couples search "book a wedding DJ Cape Town" — which means they land directly on your enquiry form, ready to get in touch.
Important

Your photos don't have descriptions Google can read

Google cannot see images. It reads a hidden text label (called "alt text") to understand what each photo shows. Right now your images either have no description or use the camera file name, which tells Google nothing.

Now
PHOTO-2024-11-20-13-07-09
Fix
DJ Mat Pheiffer performing at a Cape Town wedding reception
Why this matters for bookings Google Images is a genuine traffic source for event professionals. When a couple searches "wedding DJ Cape Town photos," properly labelled images appear in image results and drive people to your site. Your developer can update all image descriptions in the WordPress Media Library in about 15 minutes.

Priority 3
Unlocking Google's enhanced features
Important

Your business isn't registered in Google's structured data system

Google has a system called "structured data" (or schema markup). It's invisible code your developer adds once that tells Google very specifically: what type of business you are, where you're based, what you charge, and how to contact you.

What this unlocks for you Once added, your search listing could show your price range (R6,000–R17,500), your phone number, your Cape Town location, and star ratings — all visible in Google results before someone even visits your site. This is a one-time setup that takes your developer about 30 minutes.
