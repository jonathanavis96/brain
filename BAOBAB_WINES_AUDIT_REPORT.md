# Baobab Wines Website Audit Report

**Client:** Baobab Wines (baobabwines.com)  
**Current Platform:** Squarespace  
**Target Market:** B2B (Cruise Lines, Airlines, Restaurants, Retail)  
**Current Positioning:** Boutique/Direct-to-consumer feel  
**Audit Date:** 2024  
**Goal:** Assess professionalism and identify gaps vs. enterprise B2B wine importer standards

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Baobab Wines has solid foundational content and a coherent brand story, but the website presents as a **mid-market or boutique operation rather than a professional B2B distributor**. The site reads more like an educational/lifestyle wine experience portal than a business-to-business wholesale platform. Key gaps include:

- No dedicated B2B wholesale section or pricing
- Missing professional credibility markers (certifications, compliance, awards)
- SEO fundamentals incomplete (missing H1 tags, thin meta descriptions)
- Limited sales infrastructure (no request for quote, lead capture for B2B)
- Experiences/tours are the highlight, not wholesale capabilities
- Navigation doesn't reflect B2B priorities

## 6. SEO OBSERVATIONS

### ✅ **What's Good**
- **Meta tags present** (og:title, og:description, og:image for home)
- **Mobile viewport meta tag** - responsive design declared
- **Google Analytics tracking** (UA-97577043-1)
- **Sitemap/RSS** - Feed present
- **Basic Schema** - WebSite schema included
- **Open Graph tags** - Social sharing meta tags present
- **Typekit fonts** - Professional font loading (not system fonts)

### ❌ **Critical SEO Gaps**

| Issue | Current | Best Practice | Impact |
|-------|---------|---|--------|
| **Home Meta Description** | MISSING | 150-160 char description | Missing 20-30% of CTR in SERPs |
| **H1 Tags** | Missing on 5/6 main pages | One H1 per page | Confuses search engines about page topic |
| **Page Titles** | Generic ("Portfolio — Baobab Wines") | Keyword-rich ("Premium South African Wine Portfolio \| Baobab Wines") | Lower ranking for relevant keywords |
| **Meta Descriptions** | Mostly empty/missing | All pages need 150-160 chars | Reduced click-through from SERPs |
| **Schema Markup** | Minimal (WebSite only) | Product, Organization, LocalBusiness | Loss of rich snippets, knowledge panel |
| **Content Depth** | Thin on key pages | 1000+ words on Distribution | Outranked by competitors |
| **Internal Linking** | Minimal explicit structure | Strategic linking between related pages | Poor crawlability, lost link equity |
| **Image Alt Text** | Likely missing on producer images | Descriptive alt tags | Loss of image search visibility |
| **Keyword Strategy** | Not evident | Target "B2B wine importer," "wholesale South African wines" | Missing search volume for commercial intent |

### **Specific Page-by-Page SEO Issues**

**Home Page:**
- Title: "Baobab Wines" (generic, not keyword-optimized)
- Description: Missing entirely
- No H1 tag

**Distribution Page:**
- Title: "Distribution — Baobab Wines" (weak)
- Missing H1
- No structured data for locations/warehouses
- Could benefit from LocalBusiness schema for warehouse locations

**Portfolio Page:**
- Title: "Portfolio — Baobab Wines" (generic)
- Missing H1
- Could have Product schema for wine listings
- Missed opportunity for wine-specific schema

**Experiences Page:**
- Only page with H1 ("South Africa Wine Tours")
- Title: "Experiences — Baobab Wines" (too broad)
- Could have Event/Tour schema

### **Missing SEO Opportunities**
- No blog/content marketing (zero articles targeting keywords like "south african wine importer," "wholesale wine distributor," "sustainable wine portfolio")
- No location pages for states (Texas, California, etc. mentioned but no dedicated pages)
- No FAQ schema (common B2B questions not addressed)
- No producer pages optimized for individual wine search (each producer page likely indexed but not optimized)

## 10. CONCLUSION & NEXT STEPS

**Baobab Wines' website has a strong brand story and authentic positioning, but is positioned as a B2C/experiential brand rather than a professional B2B wine distributor.** The platform choice (Squarespace) and page structure (emphasizing tours over wholesale) reinforce a "small business" perception that undermines credibility with large buyers (cruise lines, airlines, restaurants).

### **Immediate Priorities (0-3 months)**

1. **Create B2B Landing Page** - Dedicated wholesale section with clear capabilities, MOQs, pricing structure
2. **Fix Critical SEO** - Add H1 tags, meta descriptions, optimize titles, add schema markup
3. **Add Compliance Credentials** - Certifications section, TTB approval, compliance statements
4. **Create Resources Section** - Downloadable spec sheets, certifications, tasting notes

### **Medium-Term (3-6 months)**

5. Collect testimonials from restaurant/cruise/airline clients
6. Enhance Distribution page with detailed logistics info
7. List regional account managers
8. Create content marketing strategy (blog targeting B2B keywords)

### **Long-Term (6+ months)**

9. Evaluate platform migration to support B2B features
10. Build wholesale portal with order history, tiered pricing, account management
